No setting that
quietly does nothing.

When a broader toggle already covers a narrower one, the narrower setting indents under it and greys out — for exactly as long as the broader one is on. You can always see which switch is doing the work.

  • Hiding the whole sidebar locks trends and search beneath it
  • Hiding the timeline locks the tab-related settings
  • Turn the broader one back off and each child resumes on its own
